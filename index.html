A message for all of us

To the Friends Who Became a Part of Life

Twenty years ago, we were just school friends sitting in classrooms, sharing jokes, lunch, homework, and little secrets. We did not know then that those simple days would become some of the most precious memories of life.

Over the years, life changed. We grew up, took different paths, faced our own struggles, built our own dreams, and became different versions of ourselves. But one thing remained special — the bond we created back then.

This Friendship Day is not just about wishing each other. It is about remembering the journey, respecting the bond, and feeling grateful that after all these years, we still have memories that can make us smile instantly.

Happy Friendship Day to the people who made school life unforgettable.

Friends Since School. Friends for Life.

Thank you for being a part of this journey. Here’s to old stories, new memories, and a bond that stays timeless.
